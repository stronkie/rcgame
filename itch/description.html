RC Turbo Cup

Isometric toy car racing for the Amiga 500. Race a radio controlled toy car through oversized rooms against four rivals, collect upgrades and weapons, spend your prize money in the shop and win three cups.

Created By Mark Stronks, Stronkie Soft

Features

  • 8 smoothly scrolling isometric tracks in 32 colours: Garage Grand Prix, Living Room Rally, Garden Sprint, Toy Store Dash, Playground Pursuit, Science Lab Circuit, Construction Crash and the Championship Arena
  • Career: Amateur Cup, National Cup and World Championship, 8 races each - finish in the top 3 to go on
  • Four rivals: Speedy Sam (fast, crashes), Careful Carla (steady), Mad Max (attacks you) and Engineer Erik (grabs the upgrades)
  • Pickups: motor, tires, battery, missiles, bombs, homing missiles, cash, roll cage, turbo, shield and repair
  • Collect S-T-R-O-N-K-I-E for the next car class, $2000 and a new paint job
  • Zippers, ramps, oil, marbles, water, sand, loose batteries and electric plates; rain and wind
  • Shop between the races, practice mode, best laps, the career is saved

Requirements

Amiga 500 (or any Amiga with a 68000) with Kickstart / Workbench 1.3 and 1 MB (512K chip + 512K trapdoor). Joystick in port 2. Bootable ADF (Gotek), hard drive / CF card installer, and a WHDLoad version.
