Stronkie's RC Cup

Isometric toy car racing for the Amiga 500. Race a radio controlled toy car through oversized rooms against four rivals, collect upgrades and weapons, spend your prize money in the shop and win three cups. (Called RC Turbo Cup up to V0.4.)

Created By Mark Stronks, Stronkie Soft

Try the free demo first: the first 2 races of the Amateur Cup and practice on those 2 tracks.

New in V0.5

  • A new name: Stronkie's RC Cup - new title logo, loading picture, manual, box and disk labels
  • The STRONKIE POCKET is now a simple 4 shade grey handheld screen, like back in the day: a white road, a flat grey ground, black and white kerbs and grey cars with black outlines

New in V0.4

  • Two new tracks: the Kitchen Table Trophy (in the cups) and a secret track
  • Backwards races: the National Cup races every track backwards, the World Championship every second race; the cups have 9 races now
  • Unlockables: a gold trophy opens the secret track, gold in all three cups gives you THE GOLDEN CAR
  • Time trial: race the ghost of your best lap, the ghost is saved on the disk
  • STRONKIE POCKET: play the race on a little handheld with a 4 shade screen (SCREEN on the title)
  • A start lights gantry and big texts: LAP 2, FINAL LAP!, FINISH!, NEW RECORD!
  • Flashing zippers, waving flags, crackling electric plates; toy cars race across the title and the logo wobbles on the beat
  • Music with drums, a crowd at the finish, and the motor sings higher with every upgrade

New in V0.3

  • Much easier to get loose from the walls: your car keeps at least half its steering even when it stands still, and turns back towards the track by itself

New in V0.2

  • New cars: 15 colours each, with a dark outline, two body shades, glass, black tyres and head and tail lights
  • The computer cars no longer take the motor, tires, battery, cash or the STRONKIE letters
  • Engineer Erik now goes for the power-ups
  • Missiles, bombs and explosions are drawn into the track
  • A free demo and a print kit: manual, fold-up box and disk labels

Features

  • 10 smoothly scrolling isometric tracks in 32 colours: Garage Grand Prix, Living Room Rally, Garden Sprint, Toy Store Dash, Playground Pursuit, Science Lab Circuit, Construction Crash, the Championship Arena, the Kitchen Table Trophy and a secret track
  • Career: Amateur Cup, National Cup and World Championship, 9 races each - finish in the top 3 to go on; backwards races in the later cups
  • Four rivals: Speedy Sam (fast, crashes), Careful Carla (steady), Mad Max (attacks you) and Engineer Erik (grabs the power-ups)
  • Pickups: motor, tires, battery, missiles, bombs, homing missiles, cash, roll cage, turbo, shield and repair
  • Collect S-T-R-O-N-K-I-E for the next car class, $2000 and a new paint job
  • Zippers, ramps, oil, marbles, water, sand, loose batteries and electric plates; rain and wind
  • Time trial against your ghost, practice forwards or backwards, unlockables, the STRONKIE POCKET handheld mode
  • Shop between the races, best laps, your career is saved

Downloads

  • V0.5 (LATEST) - Hard drive / CF card: the StronkiesRCCup drawer to copy to your hard drive, double click RCCup

  • DEMO (FREE): the free demo as a disk image ADF and as a WHDLoad version (made from V0.2, still called RC Turbo Cup)

Print your own big box

Free downloads: the Instruction manual (8 pages, A5), a Box to print and fold with the system requirements (A3) and Disk labels (A4).

Requirements

Amiga 500 (or any Amiga with a 68000) with Kickstart / Workbench 1.3 and 1 MB (512K chip + 512K trapdoor). Joystick in port 2: up = gas, fire = weapon (or fire = gas, set it on the title screen).
