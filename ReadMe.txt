STRONKIE'S RC CUP  V0.5 - TEST VERSION
Created By Mark Stronks, Stronkie Soft (c) 2026

Isometric toy car racing for the Amiga 500.
Needs Kickstart / Workbench 1.3 and 1 MB (512K chip + 512K trapdoor).

START
  Boot this disk (or put it on a Gotek): the game starts by itself.
  From Workbench: double click RCCup.
  Hard drive: double click Install on this disk.

THE GAME
  Race your radio controlled toy car through oversized rooms: a
  garage workbench, the living room, the garden path, a toy store,
  the school playground, a science lab, a building site, the
  championship arena and the kitchen table. 4 cars, 4 laps. Finish
  in the top 3 to go on; 4th place costs you a spare car and you
  race again.

  Career: the Amateur Cup, the National Cup and the World
  Championship, 9 races each. In the National Cup every track is
  raced BACKWARDS, in the World Championship every second race.
  Win prize money and points, spend the money in the shop.

  Win a GOLD trophy in a cup: the secret track opens up in
  practice. Gold in all three cups: THE GOLDEN CAR (a paint in the
  shop, and a little faster).

PRACTICE
  Any track, three modes (joystick up/down):
  RACE, RACE BACKWARDS, and TIME TRIAL: alone on the track against
  the ghost of your best lap. The ghost is saved on the disk
  (RCGhost0..9) and waits for you next time.

STRONKIE POCKET
  SCREEN on the title: COLOUR or STRONKIE POCKET. In the pocket
  the race is played on a little handheld with a 4 shade grey
  LCD screen, just like back in the day.

CONTROLS (joystick in port 2)
  LEFT / RIGHT     steer
  UP               gas          (or FIRE, see CONTROLS on the title)
  DOWN             brake / reverse
  FIRE / SPACE     use your weapon (2nd button works too)
  P or ESC         pause; ESC again quits the race
  M                music on / off
  The cursor keys work as a joystick.

ON THE TRACK
  Yellow boxes: M motor, tyre, battery (permanent upgrades),
  missiles, bombs, homing missile, $ cash, roll cage, turbo,
  shield, + repair.
  Red letter boxes: collect S-T-R-O-N-K-I-E over the races. All
  eight give you the next car (Rookie Buggy, Sport RC, Turbo RC,
  Super RC, Champion RC), $2000 and a new paint job.
  Yellow zippers speed you up, ramps make you jump.
  Watch out for oil, marbles, water, sand, loose batteries and
  (in the lab) electric plates.
  The computer cars use the weapons and power-ups too, but the
  upgrades, the cash and the letters are only for you.

YOUR RIVALS
  SPEEDY SAM     fast, but crashes a lot
  CAREFUL CARLA  slow in the bends, never makes a mistake
  MAD MAX        fires at you and rams you
  ENGINEER ERIK  goes for every power-up on the track

THE SHOP
  Motor, tyres, battery, armour, a bigger weapon rack, missiles,
  bombs, spare cars and paint. Your career is saved on the disk
  (RCCup.dat) after every race.
